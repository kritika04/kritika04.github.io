Dr Kritika Lohia

Visual neuroscience · Stereopsis · Binocular vision · fMRI

Research Fellow, School of Psychology and Neuroscience, University of St Andrews.

I study the perceptual and neural mechanisms of stereoscopic depth perception and binocular visual function, using behavioural experiments and neuroimaging, with relevance to clinical vision research.

Selected peer-reviewed research

Distinct rich and diverse clubs regulate coarse and fine binocular disparity processing: evidence from stereoscopic task-based fMRI

Lohia K, Soans RS, Saxena R, Mahajan K, Gandhi TK. iScience (2024).

This fMRI study examines brain network organisation during coarse and fine binocular disparity processing, applying Granger causality and graph-based measures to characterise rich and diverse clubs. It reports differences in network organisation and decoding performance across disparity magnitudes.

Stereopsis following surgery in children with congenital and developmental cataracts: A systematic review and meta-analysis

Lohia K and colleagues. Survey of Ophthalmology (2023 issue; online 2022).

A systematic review and meta-analysis of stereopsis outcomes after surgery for congenital and developmental cataracts in children, relevant to binocular visual development, clinical outcomes and timing of intervention.
